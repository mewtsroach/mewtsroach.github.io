About

Background

I'm a senior in CMU's School of Computer Science studying Robotics. Most of what I do is software, but I like working close to the physical system and understanding how the pieces fit together.

My projects and research have taken me across perception and computer vision, SLAM and state estimation, planning, controls, autonomy, simulation, and systems integration. I've worked on aerial vehicles, autonomous racing, manipulation, agricultural robotics, and other systems where the software ultimately has to work on real hardware.

Outside of research and classes, I also build with CMU Lunabotics, Apex Buggy, and SDC Booth.
